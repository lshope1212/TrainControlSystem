BACKGROUND: SCHEDULE FORMAT
============================================================

The example Red/Green Line schedule spreadsheets use approximately this structure:

Line
Section
Block Number
Infrastructure
Train 1 Arrival Time at Station
Train 2 Arrival Time at Station
Train 3 Arrival Time at Station
...

There is one train per train column.

Most normal track-block cells in the train columns are blank.

Times are populated at:

1. the route/start block
2. station blocks along the route

IMPORTANT:

The timestamp in the train column at the route/start block should be interpreted by CTC as the train's DEPARTURE TIME / ROUTE START TIME.

DepartureTime should therefore NOT be separately entered by the dispatcher anymore.

For example conceptually:

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Line | Section | Block | Infrastructure | Train 1 | Train 2 | Train 3
----------------------------------------------------------------------
BLUE | A       | 1     |                | 12:00:00| 12:03:00| 12:06:00
BLUE | A       | 2     |                |         |         |
BLUE | A       | 3     | CROSSING       |         |         |
...
BLUE | B       | 10    | STATION B      | 12:08:00| 12:11:00|
BLUE | C       | 15    | STATION C      |         |         | 12:14:00
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Here:

Train 1 DepartureTime = 12:00:00
Train 2 DepartureTime = 12:03:00
Train 3 DepartureTime = 12:06:00

The departure time is DERIVED from the start-block cell.

Do not require every train to have a time at every station. A train may take a different branch, so blank station cells are valid.
